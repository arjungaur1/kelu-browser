# Privacy Policy for KELU Browser

**Effective Date:** November 22, 2026

Welcome to **KELU Browser** (also referred to as K.E.L.U. Browser). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your data remains secure. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

KELU is built with a **Privacy-First** approach. Most core features (especially voice processing) are designed to run locally on your device whenever possible.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We strictly limit the data we collect:

### Voice Data
KELU uses a voice-first interface.  
The core voice-to-command / voice-to-DOM processing engine is designed to run **offline** directly on your device for low latency and maximum privacy.

- We do **not** store your voice recordings on our servers by default.
- Voice data is processed locally to interpret commands (open sites, scroll, click, fill forms, etc.).

### App Usage & Diagnostic Data
We may collect limited, non-personal, anonymous information such as:
- Crash reports
- Basic performance and stability metrics
- App version and general usage statistics

This data helps us improve reliability and fix bugs. It does not include your browsing history, voice recordings, or personal content.

### Local Data (Stored Only on Your Device)
The following data stays on your device and is **not** uploaded to our servers:
- Browsing history
- Bookmarks
- Downloads
- Custom voice commands
- Form-filling preferences / trained data (if you use “Train Kelu”)
- Tabs and session state

---

## 2. Third-Party AI Services

KELU allows you to optionally connect your own third-party AI APIs (for example Gemini, Claude, or others).

- If you enable an external AI provider, the text or processed voice queries you send to that provider are governed by **that provider’s privacy policy**.
- We do **not** intercept, store, log, or sell the data that travels between your device and your chosen AI provider.
- Using third-party AI is completely optional.

---

## 3. Data Storage & Security

- Most browsing data, voice processing, bookmarks, history, and preferences are stored and processed **locally** on your device.
- We do not maintain central databases of your browsing history, bookmarks, personal preferences, or voice recordings on our servers.
- When the app downloads updates or files, they are handled through standard Android system mechanisms.

---

## 4. Permissions We Request

KELU requests the following permissions. Each is used only for the stated purpose:

| Permission | Why We Need It |
|------------|----------------|
| **Microphone** (`RECORD_AUDIO`) | To receive and process your voice commands. |
| **Internet** | To load websites and (optionally) connect to third-party AI services you configure. |
| **Network State / Wi-Fi State** | To check connectivity and optimize browsing. |
| **Location (Fine & Coarse)** | To support websites that request your location via the browser’s Geolocation API (e.g. Maps, weather, local search) and related voice commands. Location is only accessed when a website or feature requests it and after you grant permission. |
| **Camera** | To support websites and features that need camera access (e.g. web apps, image-based challenges, or future visual features). |
| **Storage / Media Access** | To save and manage downloads, and to allow the browser to access media files when needed. |
| **Notifications** | To show download progress, update availability, and important status messages. |
| **Foreground Service** | To keep voice mode and media playback working reliably while the app is in use. |
| **Request Install Packages** | To allow the app to download and install its own updates (in-app update feature). You will always see the system installation confirmation. |
| **Modify Audio Settings / Vibrate** | For better voice interaction and feedback. |
| **Wake Lock** | To keep the device awake during active voice sessions or downloads when necessary. |

We do not use these permissions for advertising tracking or to sell your data.

---

## 5. In-App Updates

KELU can check for and download newer versions of the app.  
When an update is downloaded, the `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES` permission is used only to hand the APK to the Android system installer. You always remain in control and must confirm the installation.

---

KELU Browser is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
